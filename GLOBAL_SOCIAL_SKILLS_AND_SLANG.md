# Global Metro Cities: Social Skills & Slang Guide

A comprehensive cheat-sheet for navigating the social landscapes and local dialects of major global metropolitan hubs. 

## Universal Social Skills for Global Hubs
1. **Respect the Pace**: In high-density global cities (NYC, London, Tokyo, Mumbai), walking slowly or blocking pathways is considered highly disrespectful. 
2. **Cultural Competence**: Assume diversity. Be open to different dining etiquettes, greeting norms, and communication styles.
3. **The Local Icebreaker**: In most cities, commenting on the weather, local sports, or the traffic/transit system is the safest way to establish rapport.
4. **Time Fluidity vs. Rigidity**: Know whether a city runs on "punctual time" (Switzerland, Germany) or "fluid time" (Miami, Brazil, India).

## North America

### New York City (NYC)
*   **Social Rules**: Time is money. Walk fast, stand on the right on escalators, and get straight to the point in conversations. Don't make eye contact on the subway.
*   **Slang**: 
    *   **Bodega**: Corner deli/store.
    *   **Brick**: Extremely cold weather.
    *   **Deadass**: Seriously / I speak the truth.
    *   **Schlep**: A tedious commute or carry.
    *   **YERRR**: A loud, versatile greeting.

### Washington, D.C.
*   **Social Rules**: Highly networking-focused. "What do you do?" is standard. Know your acronyms and understand that politics is the local industry.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **The Hill**: Capitol Hill.
    *   **DMV**: DC, Maryland, Virginia area.
    *   **Inside the Beltway**: Matters concerning US government insiders.
    *   **Wonk**: A policy obsessive.

### San Francisco / Bay Area
*   **Social Rules**: Casual dress code (hoodies, Patagonia vests). Early dinners and early mornings. High enthusiasm for tech, startups, and niche dietary preferences.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Hella**: Very or a lot of.
    *   **The City**: Refers strictly to San Francisco.
    *   **Yay Area**: The Bay Area.
    *   **Seed / Series A**: Startup funding terms used in casual conversation.

### Atlanta
*   **Social Rules**: Southern hospitality meets city pace. Be polite, say hello to strangers, and be prepared to bond over traffic woes.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **ITP / OTP**: Inside the Perimeter / Outside the Perimeter (I-285).
    *   **Shawty**: A term of endearment or just referring to a person.
    *   **Hotlanta**: (Note: Locals usually avoid using this).

### Boston
*   **Social Rules**: Direct and blunt. Deep intellectual spheres (near Cambridge) mixed with intense, tribal loyalty to local sports teams.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Wicked**: Very or extremely (e.g., "Wicked smart").
    *   **The T**: The MBTA subway system.
    *   **Townie**: A working-class local, often from Southie or Charlestown.
    *   **Packie**: Liquor store (Package store).

### Miami
*   **Social Rules**: Spanglish is the default. Late nights are the norm (dinner at 9 PM or later). Appearance, luxury, and vibrant fashion are highly valued.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Dale**: Go ahead / do it (popularized by Pitbull).
    *   **Cafecito**: Cuban espresso shot.
    *   **Chonga**: Working-class Latina subculture/style.
    *   **Que bola**: What's up? (Cuban origin).

## India

### Mumbai
*   **Social Rules**: "Hustle" culture. Train etiquette is survival: let people off before boarding, and move fast. Deep respect for the chaotic but punctual Dabbawala and monsoon perseverance.
*   **Slang** (Tapori/Bambaiya slang):
    *   **Bindaas**: Carefree / relaxed.
    *   **Jhakaas**: Awesome / super.
    *   **Cutting Chai**: A half-glass of strong tea.
    *   **Jugaad**: A clever, improvised hack or workaround.
    *   **Local**: The local train network.

### Delhi (New Delhi)
*   **Social Rules**: Aggressive but warmly hospitable. Show of status (cars, weddings) is common. Bargaining is an art form.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Bhaisahab / Bhai**: Brother (used for literally anyone).
    *   **Vella**: Someone with nothing to do / jobless.
    *   **Scene kya hai?**: What's the plan?
    *   **Jugaad**: (Used here heavily as well for making things work).

### Bangalore (Bengaluru)
*   **Social Rules**: IT/Tech hub of India. The ultimate icebreaker is complaining about Silk Board traffic. Cosmopolitan and pub-heavy culture. Wait out the rain in the closest cafe.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Macha / Maga**: Dude / friend.
    *   **Bob**: Another term for dude/friend.
    *   **Oota aitha?**: Have you eaten? (Kannada).
    *   **Techie**: Standard media/local term for IT workers.

### Chennai
*   **Social Rules**: Culturally rooted but welcoming. Huge respect for classical arts, cinema, and filter coffee. Small talk often revolves around the sweltering heat.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Macha / Machi**: Brother-in-law, loosely used for friend.
    *   **Thala**: Leader / Boss (popularized by MS Dhoni and Ajith).
    *   **Peter**: Someone who shows off by speaking English unnecessarily.
    *   **Orama po**: Move aside.

### Pune
*   **Social Rules**: Student and IT hub. Known for its relaxed afternoon siesta culture in the older "Peths". Strong, proud Marathi cultural roots.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Bhau**: Brother.
    *   **Kiti vajle?**: What time is it?
    *   **Katkat**: Nuisance / headache.
    *   **Peth**: Historic neighborhoods.

## Europe

### London
*   **Social Rules**: Queueing is sacred—never skip a line. Stand on the right on the Tube escalators. Apologizing when someone bumps into *you* is normal. Master dry humor and sarcasm.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Mate**: Friend/guy.
    *   **Innit**: Isn't it? (Used as a filler).
    *   **Quid**: A pound (£).
    *   **Gutted**: Devastated/disappointed.
    *   **Banter**: Teasing, playful conversation.

### Paris
*   **Social Rules**: The golden rule: Always say "Bonjour" entering a shop and "Au revoir" leaving. Keep your voice down in public. "La bise" (cheek kissing) is standard among friends.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Verlan**: Reversing syllables of words (e.g., Fou -> Ouf).
    *   **Mec / Meuf**: Guy / Girl.
    *   **Kiffer**: To like or love something.
    *   **Bobo**: Bourgeois Bohème (hipster elite).

### Switzerland (Zurich/Geneva)
*   **Social Rules**: Punctuality is absolute—arriving 5 minutes late requires an apology. Deep respect for privacy and peace (no loud noises on Sundays).
*   **Slang**: 
    *   **Bünzli**: A stuffy, rule-abiding citizen (Swiss-German).
    *   **Röstigraben**: The cultural/linguistic divide between German and French speaking regions.
    *   **Znüni**: A 9-AM mid-morning snack.

### Germany (Berlin/Munich/Frankfurt)
*   **Social Rules**: Directness is politeness; expect no sugarcoating. Strict recycling laws. Never cross the street on a red pedestrian light (Ampelmännchen).
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Kiez**: Your local neighborhood block (Berlin).
    *   **Späti**: Late-night convenience store (Berlin).
    *   **Moin**: Hello (Northern Germany).
    *   **Servus**: Hello / Goodbye (Bavaria/South).
    *   **Geil**: Cool / Awesome.

### Portugal (Lisbon/Porto)
*   **Social Rules**: Relaxed pace. Coffee (bica) is a daily ritual. Dinner is late. Huge respect for family and national history.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Fixe**: Cool (pronounced "feesh").
    *   **Pá**: Man/guy (used as filler).
    *   **Saudade**: A deep, melancholic longing for something/someone.
    *   **Imperial / Fino**: Draft beer (Lisbon / Porto).

## Asia & Middle East

### Shanghai
*   **Social Rules**: Cash is obsolete; have WeChat/Alipay ready. Giving/saving "Face" (Mianzi) is crucial. Highly digital, very fast-paced, and blunt in business.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Maidan**: Bill, please.
    *   **Wai mai**: Food delivery.
    *   **996**: Working 9 AM to 9 PM, 6 days a week.
    *   **Dama**: Active, middle-aged women.

### Seoul
*   **Social Rules**: Deep respect for age/hierarchy (bowing, honorifics). When pouring drinks (like Soju), pour for others, never yourself. Everything happens fast ("Pbali-pbali" culture).
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Daebak**: Awesome / Jackpot.
    *   **Hul**: OMG / disbelief.
    *   **Fighting! (Hwaiting)**: Good luck / You can do it!
    *   **Chi-maek**: Fried chicken and beer.

### Turkey (Istanbul)
*   **Social Rules**: Hospitality is king. If offered tea (Çay), accept it. Street cats are deeply respected and taken care of by the community.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Kanka**: Bestie / close friend.
    *   **Eyvallah**: Thank you / Goodbye / I appreciate it.
    *   **Hadi**: Come on / let's go.
    *   **Abi / Abla**: Older brother / sister (used respectfully for slightly older strangers).

### Dhaka
*   **Social Rules**: Unpredictable mega-traffic defines schedules; being late due to traffic is fully accepted. Extreme hospitality. "Adda" (hours of aimless, intellectual or casual chatting) is a cultural pillar.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Mama**: Uncle, but used for friends or street vendors.
    *   **Para nai**: No tension / No problem.
    *   **Jhotpot**: Quickly.
    *   **Adda**: Hanging out and conversing.

## Latin America & Russia

### Mexico City (CDMX)
*   **Social Rules**: Very polite Spanish forms ("Mande" instead of "Qué" when you don't hear something). The main meal (Comida) is heavy and eaten between 2 PM and 4 PM. 
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Güey / Wey**: Dude / bro.
    *   **Chido / Padre**: Cool.
    *   **No manches**: No way / You're kidding.
    *   **Neta**: The truth / Really?
    *   **Chilango**: Someone from Mexico City.

### Brazil (São Paulo / Rio de Janeiro)
*   **Social Rules**: High physical affection (hugs, cheek kisses). Fluid concept of time ("Brazilian time" means 30 mins to 2 hours late). Football is religion.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Cara**: Guy / dude.
    *   **Beleza?**: All good? / Beautiful?
    *   **Legal**: Cool.
    *   **Top**: Awesome (Very common in SP).
    *   **Maneiro**: Cool (Rio).

### Peru (Lima)
*   **Social Rules**: Gastronomic pride is massive—complimenting the food is a sure way to win hearts. Traffic is chaotic, requiring patience. Friendly and polite interactions.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Pata**: Friend / guy.
    *   **Chamba**: Work / job.
    *   **Bacán**: Cool / awesome.
    *   **Al toque**: Right away / immediately.
    *   **Chela**: Beer.

### Moscow
*   **Social Rules**: Smiling at strangers without reason is often viewed as insincere or suspicious. Deep, philosophical conversations are preferred over superficial small talk. Exceptional hospitality behind closed doors.
*   **Slang**:
    *   **Brat / Bratan**: Brother / bro.
    *   **Kruto**: Cool.
    *   **Chuvak**: Dude.
    *   **Tusa**: Party / gathering.
    *   **Davay**: Come on / Let's go / Bye.
